# Known issues

Defects and open items that reached a run, with what found them and what would close them.
A record is kept even after the fix, because the way it escaped is the lesson.

## The shared environment could not be built from `main` — FIXED by a host-level lock, 2026-11-09

The seven plugins that share one environment (abundance, cellchat, cellcycle, de, decoupler,
liana, pseudotime) declare ranges. Nothing recorded what those ranges had resolved to, and the
environment's name hashed the ranges. So a fresh prefix had to resolve the ranges, and on the
cluster pip could not: `resolution-too-deep`, backtracking through pertpy's arviz 1.x split
(harness ADR-0027).

Measured on the workstation: uv solves the same ranges in 25 s. The group can only hold pertpy
1.0.x, because pertpy 1.1-1.4 need numpy 2 through arviz-base and four members cap numpy below 2,
and pertpy 1.5 needs Python 3.13. Even that solution cannot run abundance, for three reasons:

- pertpy 1.0.5 with statsmodels 0.15 raises ImportError;
- pertpy 1.0.5 imports `check_use_raw` from scanpy, a name scanpy first has in 1.12 (1.11.0-1.11.5
  have only `_check_use_raw`), and the group caps scanpy below 1.11;
- numpyro 0.22 cannot initialise scCODA.

Fixed in the host, with no plugin edited:

- `scprofile lock` resolves the group once, under three measured host constraints
  (`setup/locks/constraints.txt`).
- `setup/locks/x86_64-manylinux_2_17/` records 161 exact versions.
- `install` applies them with `pip --no-deps`, checks the result, and refuses a stale lock.

On an exact macOS replica of those 161 versions, the selftests of de, abundance, cellcycle,
decoupler, liana and pseudotime pass. The acceptance check is a fresh-prefix build of the group on
the cluster, followed by every member's selftest: harness `autodev/waves/W002.md`.

## The shipped lock's wheels need glibc 2.28, not the 2.17 its tag names — OPEN, 2026-11-09

The lock was resolved with `--python-platform x86_64-manylinux_2_17`. uv accepts a version for that
target when it has a wheel for glibc 2.17 **or a source distribution**. Nine pinned versions ship
cp312 linux wheels only for a newer glibc (measured from PyPI, 2026-11-09):

- glibc 2.28: h5py 3.16.0, pyarrow 26.0.1;
- glibc 2.27: contourpy 1.3.3, ml-dtypes 0.5.4, pillow 13.0.0, scikit-learn 1.9.1, tensorstore 0.1.86;
- glibc 2.24: pandas 2.3.3, scikit-misc 0.5.3.

On a machine with glibc 2.17 to 2.27, `lock.accepts` reads the tag as the floor and accepts the
lock. pip then tries to compile those nine packages from source. The real floor is glibc 2.28. The
three pins with no wheel at all (docrep, blitzgsea, loompy) are pure Python and build anywhere.
Closing it: re-lock for `x86_64-manylinux_2_28`, or resolve wheels only for the target, once the
cluster's glibc is on record (harness gap GAP-C27).

## The conda half of an environment is not locked — OPEN, 2026-11-09

A lock pins the pip closure only. The conda specs a plugin declares, such as cellchat's `r-base=4.3`
and its fifty unversioned `r-*` packages, are still solved when the environment is built, so two
builds months apart can hold different R packages or BLAS. Every build now writes
`.scprofile_resolved.txt` beside the environment, with its `conda list --export`. The difference can
therefore be read after the fact, but it is not prevented. Closing it needs an explicit conda lock
per platform, for example from micromamba or conda-lock, applied by `install`.

## The network layout is not seeded — OPEN, measured 2026-09-06

Two `cellchat` runs on identical inputs, the same `seed.use = 1L`, and identical numeric tables
(every per-unit CSV, row for row) produced a different `cellchat_net_embedding.csv` in every
one of 21 units. The embedding is the 2-D layout of the signalling-network similarity
(`netEmbedding`, UMAP over the pathway similarity); it is drawn on and never read, so no number
in a report moves — but a figure that cannot be regenerated byte-for-byte is a figure whose
review ledger (`FIGURE_REVIEW.jsonl`, keyed on the image digest) goes stale on every rebuild,
and the reuse layer cannot tell a redrawn panel from a changed one.

Found by the interface-change reproduction (the project's stage-4 RUNLOG, 2026-09-06). The fix
is a seed handed to `netEmbedding`'s UMAP, declared as config so it appears in the run's own
parameters; until then the layout is compared and reported, never judged, by any reproduction.

## `cores` and `cost` are required and checked by nothing — OPEN, measured 2026-09-10

`sch dev convert status` prints, on every kernel, which of the point's seventeen required keys a
conversion stage fills. Eleven do. Of the six that do not, four are checked elsewhere —
`scprofile validate` refuses a kernel whose `api` the host does not implement, walks `provides`
against the capability graph, and reads `state_version` and `wraps` — and `wraps` is in any case
the conversion's INPUT and cannot be its output.

**`cores` and `cost` are checked by nothing, anywhere.** The ladder's declaration tier asserts they
are PRESENT; no mechanism asks whether they are true. A kernel can declare `cores: 4` and
`cost: medium` and be scheduled on that forever. `memory_gb_base` and `memory_gb_per_100k` are not
in that position: every run measures each instance's own process tree, `capacity --memory` fits
both terms and exits 0 only when the declaration is at or above the fit, and `--declare` writes
the fit into the plugin (the `measure` stage's `apply:`, harness ADR-0018). That is the shape this
would take — a `--cores` mode reading each instance's recorded core share and wall time out of
`report.json`, a test-phase stage over it, and an `apply:` that writes what it measured.

Found by auditing the maker's coverage rather than its completeness: `build: 7 of 7 complete` was
being printed over eight required keys no stage mentioned.

## The test loop is blocked at station 6b — OPEN, measured 2026-09-10

`setup/loop.pbs`, measured that day: **43 `text_overlap` across five panels** — F1_database_coverage 18,
F2_population_power 14, F10_pathway_similarity 9, F9_patterns 1, F6_signaling_roles 1. Stations 7
(eye, 0 of 149), 8 (paper) and 9 (required outputs) are blocked behind it.

PRE-EXISTING AND MEASURED AS SUCH: three earlier runs carry the identical audits and
audit keys, so the round that moved cellchat's drawing protocol into a generated companion
neither caused these nor cleared them. They are mechanical defects `emit_figure` records on every
panel of every run and they need no eye to fix.

Found on the first run of the loop under this project's own rules. Until 2026-09-10 there was no
way to run it: DEVELOPMENT.md says the loop is not optional, the site rule is that every tool
invocation goes through the scheduler, and no job existed that did both.

## The composed section routes a total-strength sentence to the count figure (closed 2026-09-13, harness ADR-0020 step 3)

Found by the reviewer of harness blind 0006 (2026-09-13). The composed `PAPER.<plugin>.md`
attributes the range of total interaction STRENGTH ratios to Fig. 1,
which the paper's numbering gives to `nativecmp_compareInteractions_count` - a different
quantity - while the strength figure is Fig. 2. The route for the `how_much_total` need
(`figs_for(label, ("how_much_total", ...))` in `compose.py`) takes the first figure declared to
answer it, and both the count and the weight plates answer it. A writer who trusted the composed
citation had a claim withdrawn for exactly this. Same class as the note in `compose.py` about a
total citing the per-pair network. Fixed: a sentence about a quantity cites the plate whose stem names that quantity first, and a
stem naming `count` last where the quantity is not a count (`compose._figs_for`, `prefer`/`avoid`).
